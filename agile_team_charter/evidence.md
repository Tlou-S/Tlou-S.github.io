## 1. Team Purpose & Vision

**Mission** — We help youth gain quality work experience by connecting them with SME hosts through AI task matching and routing.

**Success looks like:**
- A working Host/SME flow (register, log in, post and manage tasks) that integrates with the Youth side.
- The app running locally end to end: Expo front end plus Flask/SQLite backend.
- Work reviewed by the team lead and demonstrated at the hackathon.

**Our responsibility:** the YES Quality Work Experience app. I own the **Host/SME side**; a teammate owns the **Youth side**.

## 3. Ways of Working

- ☒ **Scrum-style, 1-week sprints**
- ☐ Kanban

| Ceremony | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Progress update | Daily / as requested by the team lead | Share what is done, what is next, and blockers |
| Sprint Planning | Start of each week | Choose backlog items for the week |
| Review / Demo | End of each week (and the hackathon) | Show working features and get feedback |
| Retrospective | End of each week (15 min) | What went well, what did not, one improvement |

## 4. Working Agreements

- Confirm with the team lead **before pushing** to shared branches.
- Work on the agreed branch only, and keep Host and Youth work separated to avoid conflicts.
- Raise blockers early, no later than the next progress update.
- Ask before making scope changes (for example, adding login).
- Log work daily so the logbook stays accurate.

## 5. Communication

- **Day to day:** WhatsApp group/chat for coordination and progress updates
- **Formal:** email; in-person or call for reviews
- **Response times:** urgent blockers the same day; other questions within 24 hours

## 6. Decision-Making

- ☐ Consensus
- ☒ **Team lead (Product Owner) final decision**, with input from the team

**Escalation:** unresolved technical or scope questions go to Shaun; academic requirements go to the WIL lecturer.

## 7. Definition of Done

- Feature meets the acceptance criteria
- Runs locally (Expo web + Flask backend) without errors
- Reviewed or confirmed by the team lead
- Committed to the agreed branch (pushed only after confirmation)
- Logbook and backlog status updated

## 8. Core Values & Behaviours

- **Respect** — value teammates' time and the Host/Youth split.
- **Transparency** — report progress and delays honestly.
- **Accountability** — own my tasks and update the team without being chased.
- **Learning mindset** — treat the new codebase, tools and feedback as chances to grow.
