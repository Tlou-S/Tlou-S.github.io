# Agile Team Charter — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../) · [View Evidence](evidence.html)

## What I produced

A team charter for my placement at Soluon. It defines our purpose, the Host/Youth split, our one-week sprints, working agreements, a Definition of Done and the main risks.

## What I learned

A charter must describe how work really gets decided. In my first days I was unsure what to do after cloning the repo, and I started on the wrong branch. That showed me why working agreements matter: "confirm before pushing" and "use the agreed branch" now sit in the charter. Decision-making follows a Product Owner model because my team lead, Shaun, sets priorities, for example directing me to the Host/SME side and approving the login feature.

## Challenges

The team is small, so parts of the template built for large student teams (voting, consensus) did not apply. I also joined an existing project, so much of my context came from a README and a problem statement rather than a kickoff.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the charter with Shaun in my first week instead of drafting it afterwards, and revisit it at every retrospective so it stays accurate.
