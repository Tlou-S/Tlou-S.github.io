# Product Backlog — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../) · [View Evidence](evidence.html)

## What I produced

A prioritised product backlog of nine items with MoSCoW priority, story points and a status for each.

## What I learned

Prioritising means deciding what has to come first, not just what is most exciting. Login and task posting are "Must" because matching and tracking cannot work without hosts and tasks in the system. Shaun's instruction to focus on the Host/SME side also helped me see that a backlog is shaped by the Product Owner as well as by me.

## Challenges

Estimating points was hard because I was new to the codebase and did not yet know how long things would take. The Host and Youth sides depend on each other, so a few priorities may shift as Inam's work progresses.

## What I would do differently

I would review and re-estimate the backlog with Shaun at every sprint planning, and add a "Could" tier early so that nice-to-have ideas do not distract from the essentials.
