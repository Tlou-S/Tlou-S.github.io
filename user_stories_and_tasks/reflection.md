# User Stories and Tasks — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../) · [View Evidence](evidence.html)

## What I produced

Nine user stories in three epics: getting started, the Host/SME side, and team and WIL admin. Each has acceptance criteria and tasks, and I numbered them US-01 to US-09 so the same IDs run through the backlog, sprints and logbook.

## What I learned

Writing from the host's point of view made me think about why a feature exists, not just how to code it. The login story is a good example: it came from the need for hosts and youth not to re-register and for profiles to be protected, and Shaun approved it. Splitting stories into tasks also made big features, such as AI matching, feel manageable.

## Challenges

I joined an existing project, so I had to infer stories from the problem statement and README. Matching depends on the Youth side, so some tasks need agreement with Inam before I can finish them.

## What I would do differently

I would write acceptance criteria together with Shaun at the start of each sprint, and break the matching story into smaller stories so progress is easier to show.
